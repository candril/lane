# The README demo: hold-seconds | keys | keycap | caption. Rendered by `just demo-gif`
# (scripts/demo.sh). The keycap and caption are drawn on a panel low over the frame, so
# every step says which keys were pressed and what they did. Give every step a caption —
# a step without one drops the panel, and it reads as a glitch.
# Starts on the Team Board, board view, grouped by parent.

2.5 |                    |       | your Jira board in the terminal — lanes by parent, columns from your config
2.5 | g n                | g n   | flatten the lanes: one column per status
1.2 | j                  | j k   | h j k l walks the board — one press, one card
1.2 | l                  | h l   | …sideways too, into the next column
3.0 | L                  | ⇧L    | moves the card right: In Progress → In Review, a real Jira transition
2.5 | L                  | ⇧L    | again — In Review → Done
2.5 | H                  | ⇧H    | and back out of Done
2.5 | J                  | ⇧J    | re-ranks the card inside its column (⇧K goes up)
2.5 | S                  | ⇧S    | picks a status directly, from anywhere
1.5 | Escape             | esc   | backs out of any prompt
2.5 | a                  | a     | reassigns — edits are optimistic, reverted if Jira says no
1.5 | Escape             | esc   | back to the board
1.5 | h k                | h k   | over to the parent story
3.0 | Enter              | ↵     | opens the issue: fields, description as Markdown, children
3.0 | z h                | z h   | unfolds the history — who changed what, when
1.5 | Escape             | esc   | back to the board
2.5 | g r                | g r   | lanes the board by sprint (g p by parent, g t by type)
2.5 | g n v g            | v g   | trays each parent's sub-tasks in a basket
2.0 | /                  | /     | narrows what is loaded…
3.0 | @me Space -is:done |       | …with one small query language
1.5 | Escape Escape      | esc   | back to the board
2.0 | :                  | :     | runs the same grammar against all of Jira
3.0 | order              |       | …a word, an issue key, or raw JQL
1.5 | Escape             | esc   | back to the board
3.0 | C-p                | ^P    | lists every action available right now, with its key
1.5 | Escape             | esc   | back to the board
4.0 | ?                  | ?     | shows the whole keymap. Nothing has to be memorised.
